Graph Lab – enhanced design (working prototype)

Switch the investigation, try the 4 kinds of graph, tap a bar to read it, then answer with a suggested idea (tap or drag) or your own words.

Prof comment 2: what changed

  • Bigger, bold, dark text: axis numbers and words 17–18 px bold navy (today ≈ 12 px light grey), axis titles 19 px, value labels 18 px with a white halo.
  • Words, not only numbers: “9 hours”, “20 min”, “6 kids”, “Football”; the axis title says what it is (“Hours of sleep”, “Number of kids”).
  • Size vs count (my guess: the value measured vs how many kids): the two axis titles are colour-coded pills, WHAT: blue and HOW MANY: yellow. Tap any bar and the helper shows the same two colours: [9 hours] → [6 kids] and a full sentence. Please confirm this is what Prof meant.
  • Better line chart: thick line, big dots, light filled area, labels on every point, the highest point in gold. For word categories (games) a line is misleading, so we draw dots on stems instead and show a “Careful!” note.
  • Pie: every slice is named (“Football · 9 kids”) so colour is not the only clue. Dot plot: one dot per kid.

Prof comment 3: answers you can tap or drag

  • Steps 2 and 3 show 3 suggested ideas built from the chart’s real numbers. Tap one (works on Chromebook/tablet) or drag it into the box. Then he can edit it, or clear it and write his own.
  • A small tag shows where the text came from: 🧑 your own words · 💡 from a hint · ✏️ hint, edited by you. This can be logged as a new column (decision with Prof: what counts for stars, 10-letter rule, badges).
  • No star is lost for using ideas. Suggestions are computed with stats.js / chart data; never hard-coded.
