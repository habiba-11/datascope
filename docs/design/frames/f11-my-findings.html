My findings pop-up – enhanced design

Opens in Evidence Board before the child writes his claim. Switch the kind of investigation, the mind-change, and how it was opened.

What changed from today

  • Today: one plain list, “icon label: text”, every line looks the same, numbers hidden in a sentence.
  • Now 3 clear sections: ❓ Our question · 🔢 The data · 🧠 My thinking.
  • The data is drawn: games = bars with word labels and a 🏆 on the most common; numbers = 4 big tiles (Mean, Median, Mode, Range) with a one-line meaning and a number-line; two classes = side by side, with a note when the mean is the same but the spread is not.
  • My thinking is a timeline of his own words: first guess → changed mind (from → to, because) → what the graph shows → what it can’t show.
  • Bigger text (20 px+), bold, dark; word labels everywhere (matches Prof comment 2).

Why it helps

  • He sees his proof numbers at a glance while writing the claim, without remembering them.
  • The same numbers appear as chips in “Pick your proof”, so he can match them.
  • Reopen button label: “✍️ Let’s write my claim” (auto) or “↩ Back to my claim” (opened by the 📋 button).
