End-of-phase summary pop-up – enhanced design

Pick a phase, change the stars, press Replay. Same pop-up after all 7 phases; only the colour, texts and "Your work" change.

What is new (numbers on the pop-up)

  1. Header in the step colour, Pip, bigger title, "Step 3 of 5".
  2. Stars bigger, plus a word line ("Great thinking!") so stars are never the only signal.
  3. Your work: the child's own words or choice, shown back to him. Small chip says where it came from (own words, hint, edited hint). Neutral, never a penalty.
  4. You did great: max 3 wins, 19px, tick icons (not emoji).
  5. Next time: one tip, yellow.
  6. I learned: Pip's one-sentence takeaway, blue.
  7. Trail: 7 dots, this phase lights up, next stop lock → open.
  8. Button: one green main button only. The room summary lives as a tab in the room (Prof comment 6), not here.

Build notes

  • Files: js/feedback-card.js, css/modal.css, helpers/feedback.js (add yourWork + source to each card).
  • Your work is read from the log (the child's own question, guess, answer). Show it with textContent only. If empty, hide the box.
  • Source chip values: "your own words", "a hint", "a hint you changed". Stars never depend on it unless the rule is about reasoning words (decide with Prof).
  • Star words: 3 = "Great thinking!", 2 = "Good thinking!", 1 = "Nice start!". Never "bad".
  • Keep "Stars are for great thinking, not speed." as small print under the star words.
  • Phone / small Chromebook: pop-up scrolls inside (max-height 90vh); the button row stays pinned at the bottom.
  • Focus goes to the main button; Esc closes (= Look again). role="dialog", aria-labelledby.
  • Only one button. The last phase (Conclude) shows Finish and the all-done trail.
  • Text sizes: titles 28–32, body 19–21, small print 16. Buttons ≥ 52px high.
