Chance Lab – enhanced 3D look (working prototype)

Pick a game, say how likely, then flip / roll / spin 10, 50 or 500 times. Results here are random, just for the demo.

What changed

  • Real-looking objects instead of flat emoji: a thick gold coin (owl head / feather tail), a white die with pips, a glossy spinner with a metal needle. They float gently on the card and cast a soft shadow.
  • Lab table stage for the test: the device is big (≈190 px), the result is a big word line: “Heads! 🎉”.
  • Real motion: the coin flips in an arc, the die tumbles to the right face, the needle spins and slows down inside the slice (same needleAngle() maths as now).
  • Run sizes as 3 big round dials (10 · 50 · 500) with the word “times”.
  • Recent results row: small coloured tokens for the last 12 results, so he SEES what happened.
  • Should vs did, per outcome (not two far-apart columns): one card per outcome, striped bar = should, solid bar = did, both with BIG bold % and words.

Prof comment 7: the numbers in ( )

  • In the code, the bracket number in the “did” bar is how many times it happened: 70% (7) means 7 times out of 10. In the “should” bar the bracket is the rounded %: 1 in 2 (about 50%).
  • New: no brackets. “Did” shows 70% big, then 7 of 10 times in words underneath. “Should” shows 50% big and 1 in 2 underneath.
  • Numbers are 28 px, bold, dark; labels are 18 px+. (Please confirm this is what Prof meant.)
